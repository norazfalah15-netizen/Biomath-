Lecture Question · Ex 4
The average adult takes about 12 breaths per minute. As a patient inhales, the volume of air in the lung increases. As the patient exhales, the volume of air in the lung decreases. For t in seconds since start of the breathing cycle, the volume of air inhaled or exhaled since t=0 is given, in hundreds of cubic centimeters, by A(t)=−2cos(52π​t)+2.
Volume of air, A (hundreds of cm³), against time t (seconds)
  • i. How long is one breathing cycle?
  • ii. Find A′(1) and explain what it means.
Answeri. 5 seconds   ii. A′(1)≈2.39 hundred cm3/sec
Full solutioni. B=52π​,   P=B2π​=5 seconds​
ii. A′(t)=−2(−sin(52π​t)⋅52π​)+0=54π​sin(52π​t)
A′(1)=54π​sin(52π​)≈2.39 hundred cm3/sec
One second after the cycle begins, the patient is inhaling at a rate of about 2.39 hundred cubic centimeters per second, that is 239 cm3/sec.

Textbook Question · Problem 29
On July 7, 2009, there was a full moon in the Eastern time zone. If t is the number of days since July 7th, the percent of moon illuminated can be represented by H(t)=50+50cos(15π​t).
  • (a) Find H′(t). Explain what this tells us about the moon.
  • (b) For 0≤t≤30, when is H′(t)=0? What does this tell us about the moon?
  • (c) For 0≤t≤30, when is H′(t) negative? When is it positive? Explain what positive and negative values of H′(t) tell us about the moon.
Answer(a) H′(t)=−310π​sin(15π​t)   (b) t=0,15,30   (c) Negative on 0<t<15, positive on 15<t<30
Full solution(a) H′(t)=−310π​sin(15π​t): the rate (percent per day) at which the illuminated part of the moon is changing.
(b) H′(t)=0 at t=0, 15, 30 days: full moon (t=0, 30) and new moon (t=15); the percent illuminated is not changing at these moments.
(c) H′(t)<0 for 0<t<15: the illuminated part is shrinking. H′(t)>0 for 15<t<30: it is growing.
