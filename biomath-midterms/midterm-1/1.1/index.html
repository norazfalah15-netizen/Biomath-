Answer11

Lecture Question · Ex 6
The value of a car, V, is a function of the age of the car, a, so V = g(a).
i. Interpret the statement g(5) = 9 in terms of the value of a car if V is in thousands of dollars and a is in years.
AnswerA 5-year-old car is worth $9,000
Full solutionThe input 5 is the age (years) and the output 9 is the value (thousands of dollars): when the car is 5 years old, its value is 9 thousand dollars.
ii. In the same units, the value of a Honda is approximated by g(a) = 13.78 − 0.8a. Find and interpret the vertical and horizontal intercepts of the graph of this depreciation function g.
AnswerVertical 13.78: a new car is worth $13,780; horizontal a ≈ 17.2: the car is worth $0 after about 17.2 years
Full solutionVertical intercept: g(0) = 13.78 − 0.8(0) = 13.78 → a new Honda (age 0) is worth $13,780. Horizontal intercept: 13.78 − 0.8a = 0 → a = 13.78 ÷ 0.8 ≈ 17.2 → the car is worth $0 after about 17.2 years.

17
Lecture Question · Ex 4
All snow tree crickets chirp at essentially the same rate at the same temperature. The chirp rate, C, in chirps per minute, can be computed using the formula C = f(T) = 4T − 160, where T is the temperature in °F.
a. Solve f(T) = 0, and interpret the result.
AnswerT = 40°F: at 40°F the crickets stop chirping
Full solution4T − 160 = 0 → 4T = 160 → T = 40. At 40°F the chirp rate is 0: the crickets do not chirp.
b. Plot the function. Which description matches the graph?
AnswerAn increasing straight line through (40, 0) and (100, 240)
Full solutionC = 4T − 160 is linear with a positive coefficient (4), so it is an increasing straight line. It crosses the T-axis at T = 40 (C = 0), and at T = 100: C = 4(100) − 160 = 240. Plot (40, 0) and (100, 240) and join them.
