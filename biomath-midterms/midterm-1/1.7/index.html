Lecture Question · Ex 1
The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) recently investigated a spill of radioactive iodine. The radiation level at the site was about 2.4 millirems/hour (four times the maximum acceptable limit of 0.6 millirems/hour), so the EPA ordered an evacuation of the surrounding area. The level of radiation from an iodine source decays at a continuous hourly rate of k = −0.004.
(a) What was the level of radiation 24 hours later?
AnswerP = 2.18 millirem/h
Full solutionP₀ = 2.4, k = −0.004
P = P₀ekt ⇒ P = 2.4e−0.004t
a) t = 24, find P
P = 2.4e(−0.004)(24)
= 2.18 millirem/h
(b) Find the number of hours until the level of radiation reached the maximum acceptable limit, and the inhabitants could return.
Answert = 346.6 hours ≈ 15 days
Full solutionb) P = 0.6, find t
0.6 = 2.4e−0.004t (÷ 2.4)
ln e−0.004t = ln(0.6/2.4) [(−0.004t) ln(e), ln e = 1]
−0.004t = ln(0.6/2.4) (÷ −0.004)
t = ln(0.6/2.4) / (−0.004)
t = 346.6 hours (÷ 24) ≈ 15 days
After 15 days, the radiation level will reach the maximum accepted level.

Lecture Question · Ex 2
The population of Kenya was 19.5 million in 1984 and 39.0 million in 2009.
Assuming the population increases exponentially, find a formula for the population of Kenya as a function of time.
AnswerP = 19.5e0.028t
Full solution1984 ⇒ t = 0, P₀ = 19.5
2009 ⇒ t = 25, P = 39.0
P = P₀ekt, find k
P = 19.5ekt
t = 25 ⇒ 39 = 19.5ek(25) (÷ 19.5)
ln e25k = ln(39/19.5)
25k = ln(39/19.5) (÷ 25)
k = ln(39/19.5) / 25
k = 0.0277 (× 100%) = 2.77% per year (continuous growth rate)
P = 19.5e0.028t

To find a: a = ek ⇒ a = e0.0277 = 1.028
To find r: a > 1 ⇒ exponential growth
a = 1 + r ⇒ r = a − 1 (growth rate)
r = 1.028 − 1 = 0.028 (× 100%) = 2.8% per year

Lecture Question · Ex 6 (Doubling Time & Half-Life)
Same as Textbook Problem 23
If the quantity of a substance decreases by 4% in 10 hours,
find its half-life.
Answert ≈ 173 hours
Full solutionSolution 1:
Q = Q₀ekt
Since Q₀ is decreased by 4% ⇒ 96% remains, therefore Q = (96/100)Q₀
⇒ 0.96Q₀ = Q₀ek·10 (find k)
⇒ k ≈ −0.004
Now, t = −ln 2/k ⇒ t = −ln 2/(−0.004) ⇒ t ≈ 173 hours

Solution 2:
r = 4/100 = 0.04 in 10 hours
r = 0.04/10 = 0.004 per hour
Since the quantity is decreased: a = 1 − r = 1 − 0.004 ⇒ a = 0.996
Now, half life t = −ln 2/ln a ⇒ t = −ln 2/ln 0.996 ⇒ t ≈ 173 hours

The half-time of the substance is about 173 hours.
