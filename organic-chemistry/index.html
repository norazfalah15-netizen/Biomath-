1Isomers & Structural Isomers

Doctor · Q1
What are isomers?
الحل

Isomers are two or more compounds having the same molecular formula, hence the same molecular weight.

اشرح

الـ isomers = مركبات لها نفس الـ molecular formula (نفس عدد الذرات من كل نوع)، فيصير لها نفس الـ molecular weight.

  • CH₃CH₂OH (ethanol, liquid at room temperature, higher boiling point)

مثال: الإيثانول والـ dimethyl ether نفس C₂H₆O، بس واحد سائل والثاني غاز.

2Stereoisomers & Geometric Isomerism

Doctor · Q5
What type of isomerism exists in compounds containing "carbon-carbon double bonds" and "cyclic compounds"?
الحل

Geometric isomerism

اشرح

الـ double bond والحلقة (ring) ما يقدرون يلفون، فالمجموعات تصير على نفس الجهة (cis) أو جهتين مختلفتين (trans) ← geometric isomers.

Doctor · Q6
What is the major difference between constitutional isomers and stereoisomers?
الحل

Structural isomers have their atoms bonded in different orders, while the atoms in stereoisomers are bonded in the same order but are oriented in space differently.

اشرح

Constitutional: الترتيب نفسه مختلف. Stereo: نفس الترتيب، بس الاتجاه بالفراغ (3D) مختلف.

Doctor · Q17
List the different types of isomers encountered during your pre-class study.
الحل
  • Structural isomers / constitutional isomers
  • Stereoisomers: geometric isomers, enantiomers, diastereomers, meso compounds
اشرح

نوعين كبار: structural و stereo. وتحت الـ stereo: geometric و enantiomers و diastereomers و meso.

  2. CH₃CH=CHCH₃

اشرح

3Chirality & Chiral Carbon

Doctor · Q7
What do you understand by a chiral object and an achiral object? Give an example each.
الحل

Chiral (Greek cheir, "the hand") object cannot be superposed on its mirror image. E.g.: hand, or feet.

Achiral object can be superposed on its mirror image. E.g.: a ball, cup, conical flask.

اشرح

Chiral: صورته بالمراية ما تنطبق عليه (مثل يدك اليمين واليسار). Achiral: تنطبق (مثل الكورة أو الكوب).

Doctor · Q8
What do you understand by a chiral carbon or chiral center? Illustrate this by drawing the structure of a simple organic compound containing a chiral carbon.
الحل

A chiral carbon or chiral center is an sp³-hybridized carbon (C*) with four different atoms or groups of atoms attached to it. Also called asymmetric carbon, asymmetric center, stereocenter or stereogenic center.

A chiral molecule contains an sp³ carbon with four different groups attached. Such a molecule can exist as a pair of mirror images (enantiomers) and can rotate plane-polarized light.

اشرح

نشوف الكربون: إذا عليه 4 أشياء مختلفة ← chiral carbon (نحط عليه *). إذا فيه شيئين متشابهين ← مو chiral.

Doctor · Q14

الحل

4Optical Activity & Enantiomers

Doctor · Q9
What do you understand by the two terms dextrorotatory and levorotatory in optical rotation?
الحل

Dextrorotatory (Latin: dexter, right) refers to an enantiomer that rotates plane-polarized light to the right (clockwise rotation).

Levorotatory (Latin: levo, left) refers to an enantiomer that rotates plane-polarized light to the left (counterclockwise rotation).

Polarimeter: instrument for measuring the rotation of plane-polarized light by a chiral compound.

Optically active: a compound that rotates plane-polarized light.

اشرح

Dextro (+) = يلف الضوء يمين (مع عقارب الساعة). Levo (−) = يلف يسار. والجهاز اللي نقيس فيه اسمه polarimeter.

Doctor · Q10
List two properties (one chemical and one physical) that are different for a pair of enantiomers (mirror images).
الحل
  1. Chemical property: interactions with other chiral substances or molecules.
  2. Physical property: interactions with plane polarized light.
اشرح

الـ enantiomers تقريباً نفس كل شي، إلا شيئين: تفاعلهم مع مواد chiral ثانية، واتجاه لف الضوء.

Doctor · Q11
List four properties of enantiomers.
الحل
  • are non-superimposable mirror images
  • are optically active molecules, hence can rotate plane-polarized light in opposite directions
  • have almost identical physical and chemical properties
  • differ in their interactions with other chiral substances
  • do not have a plane of symmetry

E.g. the two forms of lactic acid (C₃H₆O₃) are a pair of enantiomers: L(+)-lactic acid is produced in muscles during exercise, while D(−)-lactic acid is found in sour milk.

اشرح

احفظي الخمس نقاط: mirror images ما تنطبق، يلفون الضوء بعكس بعض، خواصهم تقريباً نفس الشي، يختلفون مع المواد الـ chiral، وما فيهم plane of symmetry.

5Diastereomers & Meso Compounds

Doctor · Q12
Draw all possible stereoisomers for the compounds (i) and (ii) below and identify the relationship between the stereoisomers, i.e., as enantiomers, diastereomers or meso form (meso compounds).
الحل

(i) 2,3,4-Trihydroxybutanal

  1. Identify the chiral carbons: C-2 and C-3. The two chiral carbons are not similar (dissimilar).
  2. Number of stereoisomers = 2ⁿ (n = number of chiral carbons = 2) = 2² = 4 isomers.
  3. Draw one structure, then its mirror image:

Threo — identical substituents on opposite sides; Erythro — identical substituents on the same side.

A and B are enantiomers; C and D are enantiomers.
A and C, A and D, B and C, B and D are diastereomers.

Diastereomers are stereoisomers with more than one chiral carbon that are not mirror images.

(ii) 2,3-Dihydroxysuccinic acid

  1. Chiral carbons: C-2 and C-3. The two chiral carbons are similar (same 4 groups).
  2. 2² = 4 expected isomers.
  3. Draw them:

E and F are enantiomers. G and H are the same compound (meso compound, internal plane of symmetry).

So for 2 similar chiral carbons: 2 enantiomers + 1 meso form. E and meso are diastereomers; F and meso are diastereomers.

A meso compound: requires at least two stereocenters; contains an internal plane of symmetry, so the two halves cancel each other → achiral (not optically active); is symmetric about the internal mirror plane.

اشرح

الخطوات: (1) نلقى الـ chiral carbons. (2) عدد الـ isomers = 2ⁿ. (3) نرسم واحد ومرايته.

إذا الـ chiral carbons مختلفين ← 4 isomers (زوجين enantiomers). إذا متشابهين ← زوج enantiomers + meso واحد (لأن اثنين منهم طلعوا نفس المركب).

Doctor · Q16
Which of the following Fischer projections represent enantiomers (mirror images), diastereomers, meso form, and why?
الحل

Enantiomers: I and III — they are mirror images.

Diastereomers: I and II; II and III — they have more than one chiral carbon and they are not mirror images.

Meso form: II — the compound has an internal plane of symmetry and the upper and lower halves are mirror images of each other.

اشرح

نقارن الـ OH: إذا كل الـ OH انقلبت ← mirror images (enantiomers). إذا بعضها بس ← diastereomers. وإذا النص الفوق مراية النص التحت ← meso.
