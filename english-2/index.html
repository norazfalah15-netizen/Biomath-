3. Active Listening vs Empathy · Sympathy vs Empathy

Empathy: recognizing and understanding the other person's emotional state (perspective taking), and THEN responding to show we understood how they feel.

See from their view = perspective taking.

Empathy is more than just listening carefully, but in order to do it you have to be able to listen carefully or actively.

Empathy needs active listening + more.

Empathy: understand the emotional state, THEN respond (verbally or nonverbally) to show we have understood how they feel.

Understand → then respond.
