A. Documenting the chief complaint
B. Treatment of community-acquired pneumonia
C. Administration of routine immunizations (e.g., influenza, pneumococcal)
D. Correcting a supratherapeutic dose

C. Administration of routine immunizations (e.g., influenza, pneumococcal)

Preventive measures: routine immunizations (e.g., influenza, pneumococcal); patient education such as smoking cessation counseling; prophylactic measures against acute illness, including DVT prophylaxis and stress ulcer prophylaxis.

Prevention = stopping problems before they start.
