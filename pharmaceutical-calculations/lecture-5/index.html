Lecture Question · Slide 8
A prescription requires two capsules of drug X for 5/7. How many tablet should be supplied?
Number = ?

10 capsules

Number of capsules: 2 (per day)
Duration is 5/7 = 5 days
Total number of capsules: 2 × 5 = 10

4/7 = days, 2/52 = weeks, 3/12 = months.

BSA = √(Ht (cm) × Wt (kg) / 3600) (average adult = 1.73 m²). If the dose of a drug is 5 mg/m², what would be the dose for a patient measuring 165 cm and weighing 65 kg?
Dose = ?

8.6 mg

BSA = √(165 × 65 / 3600) = √2.979 = 1.73 m²
Dose = 5 × 1.73 = 8.6 mg
