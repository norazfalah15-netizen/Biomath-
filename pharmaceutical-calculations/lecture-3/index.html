Lecture Question · Slides 4–5
You need to prepare 500 ml of 45% solution. What volume of stock solution do you need which when diluted to 500 ml will give 45% solution?
Volume of stock = ? and volume of water = ?

225 ml stock + 275 ml water

1) 45% v/v means 45 ml in 100 ml
2) 45 → 100
   x → 500   x = 45 × 500 / 100 = 225 ml
3) Dilution: 500 − 225 = 275 ml of water
Formula (Slide 5): stock (ml) = % of final solution / 100 × final volume = 45/100 × 500 = 225 ml

Water = final volume − stock volume.

Student activity: 300 ml of 1 in 25 w/v solution diluted to 800 ml. What is the concentration of the diluted product in % and mg/ml?
a. % w/v = ?

1.5% w/v

1 in 25 → 1 g in 25 ml
1 g → 25 ml
x → 300 ml   x = 12 g
After dilution the 12 g stays the same, the volume becomes 800 ml:
12 g → 800 ml
x → 100 ml   x = 1.5 g → 1.5%

Dilution changes the volume, not the amount of ingredient.

b. mg/ml = ?

15 mg/ml

1.5 g → 100 ml
x → 1 ml   x = 0.015 g = 15 mg → 15 mg/ml

% w/v × 10 = mg/ml.

c. Ratio strength = ?

1 in 66.7 (≈ 1:67)

1 → x
1.5 g → 100 ml
x = 100 / 1.5 = 66.6 → 1 : 67

Ratio = 100 ÷ %.

You need to prepare 1 litre of 20% solution from 80% stock solution.
Stock volume = ? and diluent = ?

250 ml stock + 750 ml water

1) 20% → 20 ml in 100 ml
2) Units needed for 1 litre: 20 × 1000 / 100 = 200 ml
3) Stock containing 200 units: 200 × 100 / 80 = 250 ml
4) Dilution: 1000 − 250 = 750 ml water
Formula (Slide 9): stock = % final / % stock × final volume = 20/80 × 1000 = 250 ml

C1V1 = C2V2 → 80 × V1 = 20 × 1000.

Lecture Question · Slide 11
Concentrated waters (rose water, peppermint water, chloroform water) are used to produce single-strength products. They are intended for dilution in a ratio of 1 in 40.
What does "1 in 40" mean here?

1 part concentrated water + 39 parts water

1 part of concentrate is made up to 40 parts in total with water → 1 + 39 = 40

"In 40" is the total, not the water.

Extra Question · 1 part + 39 parts water
How much water must be added to 2.5 mL of concentrated peppermint water to make single-strength peppermint water (diluted 1 in 40)? What volume is produced?
Water and final volume = ?

97.5 mL water → 100 mL

1 in 40 → 1 part concentrate + 39 parts water
Water = 2.5 × 39 = 97.5 mL
Total = 2.5 × 40 = 100 mL

"In 40" is the total, not the water.

Extra Question · 1 part + 39 parts water

Concentrate = ?

25 mL

1 mL → 40 mL
x → 1000 mL   x = 1000 / 40 = 25 mL (made up to 1000 mL with water)

Concentrate = final volume ÷ 40.
