1 ml

b. Volume that contains 50 mg = ?

2.5 ml

x = 50 × 5 / 100 = 2.5 ml

50 mg is half of 100 mg, so half of 5 ml.

c. Volume that contains 75 mg = ?

7.5 ml

150 mg is 1.5 times 100 mg, so 1.5 × 5 ml.

2. Fractions

1 lecture · 16 textbook questions

Lecture Question · Slide 5
x/3 = 16/24
Find what is x.

x = 2

Denominator 24 has been divided by 8 to get 3, so numerator 16 must also be divided by 8:
x = 16 ÷ 8 = 2
Check: 2/3 = 16/24 ✓

Whatever you do to the bottom, do the same to the top.

Textbook Question · Example 2.6
Find the numerator of the fraction that is equal to 1/7 but has denominator 21.
Numerator = ?

3

Let the numerator be x: 1/7 = x/21
Denominator 7 has been multiplied by 3 to get 21, so numerator 1 must be multiplied by 3:
x = 3

Find what turns 7 into 21 (×3), then do it to the 1.

Textbook Question · Example 2.7

Numerator = ?

2

Denominator 24 has been divided by 8 to get 3, so numerator 16 must be divided by 8:
y = 2

24 ÷ 8 = 3, so 16 ÷ 8 = 2.

Textbook Question · Example 2.8

36

x = 36

Find what turns 80 into 16 (÷5), then do it to 180.

Textbook Question · Example 2.9

Lowest terms = ?

7/9

Both 35 and 45 can be divided by 5:
35/45 = 7/9

Lowest terms: no whole number (other than 1) divides both top and bottom.

5. Finding a Missing Value & Practical Problems

Textbook Question · Example 2.21
For the proportional sets:
7y910…
21242730…
Find the missing value y.

y = 8

Method A: y/24 = 9/27
24 × y/24 = 24 × 9/27 → y = 216/27 = 8
Method B (spotting): each lower value is 3 times the upper value, so y × 3 = 24 → y = 8

Method A works always; spotting is quicker when the multiplier is obvious.

6. Exponentials (Powers of 10)

Lecture Question · Slide 9
Physical and chemical measurements deal with either very small or large numbers, so for convenience we use exponentials. Positive power means multiply; negative power means divide.
a. Write 3,000,000 using a power of 10.

3 × 10⁶

3,000,000 = 3 × 1,000,000 = 3 × 10⁶ (positive power)

Count the zeros: 6 zeros → 10⁶.

b. Write 0.000003 using a power of 10.

3 × 10⁻⁶

0.000003 = 3 / 1,000,000 = 3 × 10⁻⁶ (minus power)

Count how many places the point moves to reach 3: 6 places → 10⁻⁶.

y = 100 × 1 / 0.1 = 1000
Ratio strength = 1:1000

Ratio strength is always written as 1 : something.

y = 1 × 100 / 1,000,000 = 0.0001%

ppm = parts per 1,000,000; percent = parts per 100.

8. Variation (Inverse Proportion)

1 lecture questions

Lecture Question · Slide 12
Sometimes pharmaceutical calculations deal with inverse relationships — twice the cause, half the effect — as when you decrease the strength of a solution by increasing the amount of diluent.
If 10 litres of a 5% solution are diluted to 40 litres, what is the percentage strength of the dilution?

1.25%

Method 1: 10 (litres) / 40 (litres) = x(%) / 5(%)
x = 10 × 5 / 40 = 1.25%
Method 2: C₁V₁ = C₂V₂
5 × 10 = C₂ × 40
C₂ = 50 / 40 = 1.25%

More volume → weaker solution. 4 times the volume → 1/4 of the strength (5 ÷ 4 = 1.25).
