Question 1 of 98
A drug has a concentration of 100 mg/5 ml. What volume contains 75 mg?
A. 15 ml
B. 3.75 ml
C. 1.5 ml
D. 7.5 ml

B. 3.75 ml

Given: 100 mg in 5 ml; want 75 mg.
Proportion: 100 mg / 5 ml = 75 mg / x ml
x = 75 × 5 / 100 = 3.75 ml (the same value as the 75 mg row of the lecture table below).

Less drug than 100 mg means less than 5 ml — so the answer must be under 5 ml.

Question 10 of 98
Find x if 1/5 = x/20.
A. 5
B. 0.25
C. 4
D. 100

C. 4

Cross-multiply: 5 × x = 1 × 20 → 5x = 20 → x = 20 / 5 = 4.
Check: 4/20 = 1/5 (the lecture lists 1/5 = 2/10 = 3/15 = 4/20 = 5/25).

The bottom went from 5 to 20 (× 4), so the top goes from 1 to 4 (× 4).

Question 11 of 98

B. 3/4

B. 3/4

Divide top and bottom by the SAME number, and use the biggest one that fits both.

(Or as a proportion: x = 10 × 5 / 40 = 1.25%.)

A. 5 mg/ml

D. 0.5 mg/ml

Question 5 of 124
The maximum recommended dose of beclomethasone, an aerosolized inhalant, is 168 µg administered twice daily. The commercial inhaler delivers 42 µg per metered inhalation and contains 200 inhalations. How many inhalers should be dispensed to a patient if a 60-day supply is prescribed?
A. 2 inhalers
B. 5 inhalers
C. 1 inhaler
D. 3 inhalers

D. 3 inhalers

Given: dose = 168 µg twice daily; 42 µg per inhalation; 200 inhalations per inhaler; 60 days.
Dose per day = 168 µg × 2 = 336 µg/day
Dose for 60 days = 336 × 60 = 20160 µg
One inhaler contains 42 µg × 200 = 8400 µg
Number of inhalers = 20160 ÷ 8400 = 2.4
2 inhalers are not enough (only 16800 µg), so round UP: 3 inhalers.
(Check with puffs: 168 ÷ 42 = 4 puffs per dose → 8 puffs/day × 60 = 480 puffs; 480 ÷ 200 = 2.4 → 3 inhalers.)

You cannot dispense part of an inhaler, so 2.4 inhalers must be rounded up to 3 to cover all 60 days.
