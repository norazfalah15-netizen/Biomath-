Question 1 of 98
A drug has a concentration of 100 mg/5 ml. What volume contains 75 mg?
A. 15 ml
B. 3.75 ml
C. 1.5 ml
D. 7.5 ml

B. 3.75 ml

Given: 100 mg in 5 ml; want 75 mg.
Proportion: 100 mg / 5 ml = 75 mg / x ml
x = 75 × 5 / 100 = 3.75 ml (the same value as the 75 mg row of the lecture table below).

Less drug than 100 mg means less than 5 ml — so the answer must be under 5 ml.

Question 10 of 98
Find x if 1/5 = x/20.
A. 5
B. 0.25
C. 4
D. 100

C. 4

Cross-multiply: 5 × x = 1 × 20 → 5x = 20 → x = 20 / 5 = 4.
Check: 4/20 = 1/5 (the lecture lists 1/5 = 2/10 = 3/15 = 4/20 = 5/25).

The bottom went from 5 to 20 (× 4), so the top goes from 1 to 4 (× 4).

Question 11 of 98

B. 3/4

B. 3/4

Divide top and bottom by the SAME number, and use the biggest one that fits both.

(Or as a proportion: x = 10 × 5 / 40 = 1.25%.)

A. 5 mg/ml

D. 0.5 mg/ml
