Question 1 of 80
How many moles are there in 24 g of carbon? (C = 12)
A. 2 moles
B. 0.5 moles
C. 288 moles
D. 24 moles

A. 2 moles

Given: mass = 24 g; relative atomic mass of C = 12, so 1 mole of carbon = 12 g.
Formula: moles = mass (g) / atomic mass (g per mole)

One mole of carbon weighs 12 g, so ask how many 12 g portions fit into 24 g.

Find the total mg of salt, then see how many 58.5 mg portions it contains.
