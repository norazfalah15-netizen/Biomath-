Lecture Question · Slides 3–4
One mole = the relative atomic mass in grams. One mole of any element contains 6 × 1023 atoms (Avogadro's constant). Atomic masses: Ca = 40, Cl = 35.5, Na = 23, C = 12, H = 1.
a. How many moles are in 24 g of carbon?

2 moles

12 g of carbon = 1 mole
24 g → 24 / 12 = 2 moles

Moles = grams ÷ atomic mass.

b. How many moles (and atoms) are in 1 g of carbon?

1/12 mole = 6/12 × 1023 atoms

1 g / 12 = 1/12 mole
atoms = 1/12 × 6 × 1023 = 0.5 × 1023

1 mole = 6 × 1023 atoms.

Extra Question · Moles of an element
Atomic mass: Na = 23. Avogadro's constant = 6 × 1023.
How many moles (and atoms) are in 92 g of sodium?

4 moles = 24 × 1023 atoms

23 g = 1 mole
92 / 23 = 4 moles
4 × 6 × 1023 = 24 × 1023 atoms

Moles = grams ÷ atomic mass.

Extra Question · Moles of an element
Atomic mass: Ca = 40. Avogadro's constant = 6 × 1023.
How many moles (and atoms) are in 10 g of calcium?

0.25 mole = 1.5 × 1023 atoms

10 / 40 = 0.25 mole
0.25 × 6 × 1023 = 1.5 × 1023 atoms

Moles = grams ÷ atomic mass.

Lecture Question · Slide 5
Sodium chloride (NaCl) is made up of sodium (Na) and chloride (Cl): NaCl → Na+ + Cl−.
Molecular mass of NaCl = ? and what does 1 mole of NaCl give?

58.5 g → 1 mole Na+ + 1 mole Cl−

Na 23 + Cl 35.5 = 58.5
One mole = the molecular mass in grams = 58.5 g
1 mole NaCl gives 1 mole of sodium and 1 mole of chloride

Molecular mass = sum of the atomic masses.

Extra Question · Ions from one mole
Potassium chloride (KCl): K = 39, Cl = 35.5. KCl → K+ + Cl−.
Molecular mass of KCl = ? and what does 1 mole of KCl give?

74.5 g → 1 mole K+ + 1 mole Cl−

39 + 35.5 = 74.5
1 mole KCl = 74.5 g
1 KCl gives 1 K+ and 1 Cl−

One of each ion per formula.

Extra Question · Ions from one mole
Calcium chloride (CaCl2): Ca = 40, Cl = 35.5. CaCl2 → Ca2+ + 2 Cl−.
Molecular mass of CaCl2 = ? and what does 1 mole give?

111 g → 1 mole Ca2+ + 2 moles Cl−

40 + (35.5 × 2) = 111
1 mole CaCl2 = 111 g
Cl2 → 2 moles of chloride

Count the small number after each ion.

CO2: C = 12, O = 16.
Molecular mass of CO2 = ?

44 → 1 mole of CO2 = 44 g

C = 12
O = 16 + 16 = 32
Total = 44
1 mole of CO2 = 44 g (it contains 1 mole C = 12 g and 2 moles O = 32 g)
