Lecture Question · Slide 3
Cockroft-Gault equation — write it and the units.
CrCl (mL/min) = ?

(140 − Age) × Weight × Constant / Serum creatinine

Age in years
Weight in kg (actual, ideal or adjusted — depends on BMI)
Serum creatinine in micromol/litre
Constant = 1.23 for men; 1.04 for women

AjBW (obese patients, actual weight > IBW) = IBW + 0.4 × (actual body weight − IBW). Calculate the AjBW of a woman who is 157 cm tall and weighs 80 kilograms.
AjBW = ?

≈ 61.8 kg

157 / 2.54 = 61.8 inches → 1.8 inches over 5 feet
IBW = 45.5 + 2.3 × 1.8 = 49.7 kg
AjBW = 49.7 + 0.4 × (80 − 49.7) = 49.7 + 12.1 = 61.8 kg

Lecture Question · Slide 10
Determine the loading and maintenance doses of gentamicin for a 76-year-old male weighing 190 lb with a height of 6 feet and serum creatinine of 2.4 mg/dL (212 mcmol/L). Loading dose 1.5 mg/kg of IBW; maintenance 1 mg/kg of IBW every 8 hours after the initial dose.
a. Loading and maintenance doses = ?

IBW 77.6 kg → loading 116.4 mg; maintenance 77.6 mg every 8 h

6 feet = 72 inches → 12 inches over 5 feet
IBW = 50 + 2.3 × 12 = 77.6 kg
Loading = 1.5 × 77.6 = 116.4 mg
Maintenance = 1 × 77.6 = 77.6 mg every 8 hours

The doctor asked for IBW, so use IBW (not the actual 190 lb). No answer on the slide — solved by us.

b. Extra: his creatinine clearance (Cockroft-Gault) = ?

≈ 30 mL/min

190 lb / 2.2 = 86.4 kg; 72 in = 1.83 m → BMI = 86.4 / 1.83² = 25.8 → use AjBW
AjBW = 77.6 + 0.4 × (86.4 − 77.6) = 81.1 kg
CrCl = (140 − 76) × 81.1 × 1.23 / 212 = 30.1 mL/min

Serum creatinine in micromol/L → use 212, not 2.4.

5. Adjusting CrCl for Body Surface Area

Lecture Question · Slide 12
Adjusted CrCl = BSA / 1.73 × eCrCl. If a patient weighing 120 lb and measuring 60 in. in height has a calculated creatinine clearance of 40 mL per minute, adjust the CrCl based on body surface area.
Adjusted CrCl = ?

≈ 35 mL/min

120 lb / 2.2 = 54.5 kg; 60 in × 2.54 = 152.4 cm
BSA = √(152.4 × 54.5 / 3600) = 1.52 m²
Adjusted CrCl = 1.52 / 1.73 × 40 = 35.1 mL/min

Find BSA, then scale to the average adult (1.73 m²). No answer on the slide — solved by us.
