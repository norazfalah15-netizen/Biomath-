Question 1
Which of the following cycloalkanes has the highest angle strain?
  • A) cycloheptane
  • B) cyclohexane
  • C) cyclopentane
  • D) cyclobutane
  • E) cyclopropane
Show answer
Answer: E — cyclopropane

Ring angle 60° vs 109.5° → biggest deviation → highest angle strain.

Question 2
What is the number of polar bonds in this compound?
  • A) 1
  • B) 2
  • C) 3
  • D) 4
  • E) 5
Show answer
Answer: B — 2

The two C–O bonds are polar; C–C and C–H are non-polar.

Question 3
What is A in the following reaction?
  • A) o-nitrophenol
  • B) p-nitrophenol
  • C) o-nitrophenol and p-nitrophenol
  • D) 3,4,5-trinitrophenol
  • E) 2,4,6-trinitrophenol
Show answer
Answer: E — 2,4,6-trinitrophenol (picric acid)

OH is an o/p director; 3 HNO₃ add NO₂ at both ortho and the para positions → 2,4,6.

Question 4
Which of the following structures represents di-isopropyl ether?
Show answer
Answer: A — A

Isopropyl–O–isopropyl: each side is CH(CH₃)₂ (two branches).

Question 5
Which of the following structures best represents a pair of enantiomers?
  • A) i, ii
  • B) i, iii
  • C) ii, iii
  • D) ii, iv
Show answer
Answer: D — ii, iv

ii and iv are the two trans-1,3-cyclohexanediols (mirror images). i and iii are cis = the same meso compound.

Question 6
What is the product of the following reaction?

  • C) Propanamine
  • D) Propanol
  • E) Propanone

Question 7

  • B) 2-pentene
  • C) 3-hexene
  • D) 2-methyl-2-hexene

Answer: D — 2-methyl-2-hexene

C2 carries two identical CH₃ groups → no cis/trans.

Question 8
In the following structure, how many chiral carbons and number of stereoisomers can form, respectively?
  • A) 2 and 4
  • B) 3 and 8
  • C) 3 and 6
  • D) 4 and 16
  • E) 5 and 32
Show answer
Answer: A — 2 and 4

Only the two ring carbons of the β-lactam (wedge/dash) are stereocentres → 2² = 4.

Question 9
Which of the following groups are equatorial and above the plane?
  • A) CN and Cl
  • B) C₂H₅ and SCH₃
  • C) C₂H₅ and Br
  • D) Br and SCH₃
Show answer
Answer: D — Br and SCH₃

Answer key: D. Equatorial bonds point outwards (roughly in the ring plane); look for the ones tilted upward.

Question 10
Alcohols have higher boiling points than ethers because –OH can form ……
  • A) dipole–dipole bonds
  • B) hydrogen bonds
  • C) van der Waals bonds
  • D) ionic bonds
Show answer
Answer: B — hydrogen bonds

O–H donates H-bonds to other alcohol molecules; ethers cannot.

Question 11
What is the product of the reaction of (CH₃)₂NH with acetone (CH₃COCH₃)?
  • A) diazonium salt
  • B) enamine
  • C) ammonia
  • D) imine
  • E) Schiff’s base
Show answer
Answer: B — enamine

2° amine + aldehyde/ketone → enamine (1° amine → imine/Schiff base).

Question 12
Which of the following are meso compounds?
  • A) i & ii
  • B) i & iii
  • C) ii & iii
  • D) iii & iv
  • E) i & ii & iii
Show answer
Answer: C — ii & iii

Same groups top and bottom + same groups on the same side → internal mirror plane → meso.

Question 13
What is the name of the following structure?
Show answer
Answer: 2,5-dichlorohex-5-en-3-ol

OH gets the lowest number (C3, numbering from the CH₃ end); C=C at C5; Cl at C2 and C5.

Question 14
What is the product of the following reaction?
  • A) Phenol + ethyl bromide
  • B) Bromobenzene + ethanol
  • C) Bromobenzene + ethyl bromide
  • D) Phenol + ethanol
Show answer
Answer: A — Phenol + ethyl bromide

The aryl–O bond (sp² C) is not cleaved; Br attacks the ethyl group.

Question 15
What is the name of the following structure?
Show answer
Answer: 4-ethyl-5,5-dimethyl-2-heptene

Longest chain containing C=C = 7 C; number from the end nearest C=C (C2); ethyl at C4, two methyls at C5.

Question 16
Which of the following has the highest boiling point?
Show answer
Answer: B — B (1° amine, butylamine)

1° amine has two N–H → most H-bonding here; aldehyde, 3° amine and alkane cannot H-bond to themselves; 2° amine has one N–H.

Question 17
The resolution of the racemic mixture of naproxen shown below can be achieved by:
  • A) Reacting the racemic mixture directly with an enzyme
  • B) Separation on normal silica gel column chromatography
  • C) Converting the acid to ester, then hydrolysing the ester with esterase to pure S and R enantiomer
  • D) Converting the acid to ester, reducing the ester to the alcohol and hydrolysing with esterase to give pure enantiomer
Show answer
Answer: C — Ester → esterase hydrolysis

The enzyme (esterase) hydrolyses only one enantiomer of the ester → the two can be separated.

Question 18
What is the product of the following reaction?
Show answer
Answer: Bromocyclohexane + ethyl bromide

2 equiv HBr: first cleavage gives an alcohol + alkyl bromide, the second HBr turns the alcohol into a bromide too (+ H₂O).

Question 19
Which of the following is the correct structure for 1-ethynyl-2-isopropylcyclohexane?
Show answer
Answer: A — A

Ethynyl (–C≡CH) on C1 and isopropyl on the next ring carbon (C2).

Question 20
Which of the following is the most acidic compound?
Show answer
Answer: B — B (2,4-dinitrophenol)

Two NO₂ groups (o/p, strong EWG) stabilise the phenoxide most. Alcohols (E) are the least acidic.

Question 21
Which of the following is the least basic compound?
  • A) CH₃CH₂NH₂
  • B) CH₃CONH₂
  • C) CH₃CONHCOCH₃
Show answer
Answer: C — CH₃CONHCOCH₃

Two C=O groups pull the N lone pair away (resonance) → least basic. Amine (A) is the most basic.

Question 22
What is the product of this reaction?
Show answer
Answer: CH₃CH₂CH₂NH₂ (propylamine)

LiAlH₄ reduces C≡N to CH₂–NH₂ (1° amine).

Question 23
What is the type of the following reaction?
  • A) Electrophilic addition
  • B) E1
  • C) E2
  • D) Nucleophilic substitution SN1
  • E) Nucleophilic substitution SN2
Show answer
Answer: D — SN1

tert-Butanol + HBr: OH is protonated, leaves as H₂O → stable 3° carbocation → Br⁻ attacks.

Question 24
Which of the following is the most basic?

Answer: B — B (2,4,6-trimethylaniline)

Answer key: B — three CH₃ (electron-donating) make it more basic than aniline. Note: in reality NH₃ (pKb 4.75) is a stronger base than any aniline.
