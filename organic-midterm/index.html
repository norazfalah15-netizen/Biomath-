  • B) ii, iv only

Question 4

Question 6
Which of the following about all the possible stereoisomers of 2-amino-1-phenyl-1-butanol is correct?
  • A) One pair of enantiomers, one pair of diastereomers and one meso compound
  • B) Two pairs of enantiomers and one pair of diastereomers
  • C) Two pairs of diastereomers and two meso compounds
  • D) Two pairs of enantiomers and four pairs of diastereomers
Show answer
Answer: D — Two pairs of enantiomers and four pairs of diastereomers

2 different stereocentres → 2² = 4 stereoisomers = 2 enantiomer pairs; each isomer is a diastereomer of the 2 others of the other pair → 4 diastereomer pairs. No meso (ends are different).

Question 7
Choose the correct answer (lowest to highest order of priority):
  • A) CH(CH₃)₂ < CH₂CH₂CH₃ < CH₂CH₂CH₂Br < CH₂CH₂Cl
  • B) CH₂CH₂CH₃ < CH(CH₃)₂ < CH₂CH₂Cl < CH₂CH₂CH₂Br
  • C) CH(CH₃)₂ < CH₂CH₂CH₃ < CH₂CH₂Cl < CH₂CH₂CH₂Br
  • D) CH₂CH₂CH₃ < CH₂CH₂CH₂Br < CH₂CH₂Cl < CH(CH₃)₂
Show answer
Answer: D — CH₂CH₂CH₃ < CH₂CH₂CH₂Br < CH₂CH₂Cl < CH(CH₃)₂

1st atom all C: CH(CH₃)₂ has (C,C,H) → highest. Others (C,H,H) → go to 2nd atom: CH₂Cl (Cl) beats CH₂ (C). Then CH₂Br at the 3rd atom beats CH₃.

Question 10
Which is correct?
  • A) The boiling point for branched alkanes is higher than that of straight-chain alkanes.
  • B) The boiling point increases for both of them if we decrease the number of carbons.
  • C) The molecular formula of both alkanes and alkynes is CₙH₂ₙ₋₂.
  • D) Terminal alkynes are more weakly acidic than alkanes.
Show answer
Answer: D — Terminal alkynes are more weakly acidic than alkanes

Terminal alkyne C–H (sp) is more acidic than alkane C–H. A, B, C are false (branching lowers bp; bp increases with more carbons; alkanes are CₙH₂ₙ₊₂).

Question 11
Which of the alkene starting materials will undergo ozonolysis (O₃), with subsequent treatment with H₃O⁺ and H₂O₂, to give compound Z?
Show answer
Answer: C — C (1-methylcyclohexene)

Z = HOOC–(CH₂)₄–CO–CH₃ (6-oxoheptanoic acid): one chain with a ketone (C bearing CH₃) and an acid (C that had H, oxidised by H₂O₂). Ring opens → 1-methylcyclohexene.
